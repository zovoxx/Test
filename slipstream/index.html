Tips

  • Drift: at speed, tap DRIFT while steering into a corner, then hold the gas and steer to control the slide. Counter-steer to straighten up.
  • Drifting fills your nitro faster and scores points – chain long drifts for up to a ×5 multiplier. Hitting a wall loses the combo.
  • Brake before corners; the grip is best when you're straight.
  • Pass every checkpoint in order – shortcuts don't count. A WRONG WAY warning shows if you turn around.
  • Stuck? The car resets to the last checkpoint after 3 seconds, or tap ↺.
  • Earn coins from races, drifting and Free Roam to unlock cars, paint and reverse tracks.
  • Prefer one-handed play? Turn on Auto-accelerate in Settings.
